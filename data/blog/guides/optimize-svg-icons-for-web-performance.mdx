## Why SVG optimization matters

Icon-heavy UIs can accumulate kilobytes of avoidable payload and impact render speed.

## Optimization checklist

- Remove metadata and redundant attributes
- Normalize path data where possible
- Keep viewBox and accessibility labels intact
