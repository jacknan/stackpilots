## Why timestamp bugs happen

Teams mix seconds and milliseconds, UTC and local time, and inconsistent serialization formats.

## Reliable conversion strategy

- Normalize to UTC at boundaries
- Keep internal precision consistent
- Convert for display only at the UI layer
